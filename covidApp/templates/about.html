About Us

Covid Care Speciality Hospital

Welcome to Novena Hospital, a leading healthcare institution committed to providing exceptional and compassionate care for individuals affected by COVID-19. At the forefront of the battle against the pandemic, our dedicated team of healthcare professionals is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and a patient-centric approach to ensure the well-being of every individual under our care.

Our Commitment:

In the face of COVID-19 challenges, our hospital stands as a beacon of hope and healing. Our unwavering commitment to the community is reflected in our comprehensive COVID-19 care services, prioritizing patient safety, comfort, and recovery.

Expert Medical Team:

Our skilled medical team, including specialized COVID-19 care experts, is dedicated to delivering the highest standards of care. From diagnosis to recovery, our professionals work tirelessly to ensure personalized and effective healthcare for each patient.

Comprehensive Care:

Understanding COVID-19, we provide comprehensive services: diagnostic testing, advanced medical interventions, and holistic support for well-being. Our integrated approach ensures complete patient care on their journey to recovery.

Patient-Centric Approach:

We prioritize the comfort and well-being of our patients. Our compassionate healthcare professionals are dedicated to creating a supportive and nurturing environment, fostering hope and optimism during challenging times.
